\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What the proposed method is designed to improve.}
The proposed framework is motivated by a specific failure mode of weak surgical video-text pretraining: the paired interval can be globally correct while still containing only a small subset of truly supportive frames. Our method is therefore not primarily intended to replace stronger backbones, better corpora, or richer caption hierarchies. Instead, its primary contribution is to explicitly address an under-modeled issue in existing surgical VLP---temporal imprecision inside weakly paired intervals---by changing how weak supervision is consumed during training.

\paragraph{Why training-only denoising is a deliberate design choice.}
A key design principle of this work is to separate \emph{training noise handling} from \emph{test-time inference}. Since the temporal imprecision problem is introduced by the weakly supervised pretraining data, it should be addressed where it occurs: during pretraining. For this reason, we do not carry text-guided frame selection into downstream inference. Test-time evaluation remains standard CLIP-style independent encoding of video and text, which preserves scalability, avoids query-conditioned retrieval behavior, and makes matched-backbone comparisons easier to interpret. This design also keeps the contribution focused: the paper claims better use of weak supervision during pretraining, not a more complex inference protocol.

\paragraph{Role of hierarchy in the final formulation.}
Hierarchy still plays a lightweight role in the final formulation, but it now does slightly more than provide a temperature prior. Fine/mid/coarse labels act as evidence granularity priors: fine-level supervision is expected to concentrate on fewer frames, whereas mid/coarse supervision naturally supports smoother evidence distributions over longer temporal spans. In the current implementation, hierarchy enters in three practical ways: through level-dependent frame-selection temperatures, through mixed-level batch construction that can anchor fine/mid/coarse samples from the same video, and through a lightweight adjacent-level consistency regularization on selected views. We therefore use hierarchy to shape evidence concentration, batch exposure, and cross-level coherence, while still avoiding a heavy hierarchy-specific prediction module.

\paragraph{Limitations and future directions.}
The method still depends on the quality of the paired text. If a caption is semantically incomplete, temporally drifting, or only loosely related to the local visual content, text-guided reweighting cannot fully recover the correct evidence. In particular, the present formulation addresses the temporal-alignment side of weak-pair noise more directly than commentary drift or caption corruption. Future work could study stronger but still lightweight local matching functions, better uncertainty estimates for frame selection, or curriculum schedules that vary denoising strength across training. Another useful direction is to quantify temporal noise more directly with partially refined timestamps so that denoising behavior can be evaluated against localized reference evidence rather than only downstream transfer performance.
